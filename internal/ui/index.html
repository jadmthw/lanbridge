Play your world with friends

Friends join your single-player world over the internet as if they were on your Wi-Fi. No server to rent, no mods.

  1. In Minecraft, open your world, press Esc and choose Open to LAN, then Start LAN World.
  2. Start hosting here, then send your friends the invite code.
