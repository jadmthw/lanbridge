AI players

Bring GPT, Claude, Grok or Gemini into your world as players. Everyone in the world can talk to them in chat, and they can follow you, mine, fight and hand you things.

Your world

Looking for a world…

  1. On the computer that hosts the world, install Fabric and the Carpet mod for your Minecraft version. Friends who join don't need them.
  2. Add LANBridge's script to Minecraft:
      My Minecraft folder isn't listed
    • Open your world and type /script load lanbridge in chat. LANBridge connects within a few seconds.

    Models

    Set up the ones you want. GPT and Grok can run on your ChatGPT or SuperGrok plan; Claude and Gemini need an API key. Keys stay on this computer.

    Add an AI player

    In the game you can also type !ai spawn claude, !ai remove Claude_1 or !ai list. To talk to one, say its name, like "Claude_1, come here".

    In the world

      Limits

      Every reply is a model call, so these keep your credits or plan usage in check.
